My mind thinks of a place: It exists.

2023

Installation



A Rose is a Rose is a Rose.

-Gertrude Stein


Think of a space. Any space. From anywhere. Somewhere.Now, summon it across skies and oceans, against the current of time, and bring it to mind.Consider the vast world we inhabit—and how it may exist, all at once, right here, right now, within your own perception.



My mind thinks of a place: it exists challenges the relationship between language , space and human. Through the construction of syntax, the work encourages the viewer to autonomise their agency in their relation to space and language, allowing imaginative states to emerge between worlds and become momentarily tangible. In doing so, the work interrogates the materiality of space and further destabilises the boundary between the tangible and intangible.

The work proposes that our awareness of space is articulated through language, with language itself becoming the medium through which space is constructed. Therefore, by stripping language to just prepositions and articles, the work strips the space dimension of its identity, surrendering authority to the viewer.
