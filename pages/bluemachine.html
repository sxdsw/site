Blue Machine

2026

Computational Poetry




Blue is a colour that leaves me with an empty feeling that stirs in me. How may I articulate this feeling? If I can retrieve a meaning rooted in the history of recorded blues, there has to be some truth to be sought after among all lived truths. Thus, I searched for what every written record of blue had meant.

Blue Machine is an attempt to reconcile the capabilities of the machine with the failure of my human experience to articulate my encounters with blue. Through the interactions between my machine and environment, perhaps something may emerge and my blues make take on a more definable shape.

Perhaps, with the assistance of computational methods I may be able to locate something among the words that had lived and seen through blues of centuries. Where my mind and heart cannot articulate blue, Computation then offers something governed by rules, that may guide me towards something more definable..

Blue Machine speculates a co-existence with machines, for the possibility that machines can complement where the human faculties fail. This is my attempt to hold what I cannot articulate in the company of a machine, by relying on machines to help me make meaning of the colour of infinite feeling and meaning.
